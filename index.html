12 — The Decision

Go or no-go?

Reasons to Go

  • SAR 2.3T market with structural supply gap
  • Government built tokenization rails
  • No competitor solves developer exit
  • Developer-pays = most investor-friendly
  • REGA deadline creates urgency
  • Partners provide day-1 pipeline
  • SAR 7.6M filled in 15 days by $667K startup

Reasons to Wait

  • Ghanem: 18-month head start + $7.1M
  • Requires SAR 3–5M seed capital
  • 18–24 months to first revenue
  • REGA approval not guaranteed
  • Market may consolidate
  • Dev willingness to pay 4–5% is unvalidated

The single most important next step:

Interview 10–15 Riyadh developers. Validate they'll pay 4–5%. If yes, apply to REGA sandbox by April 30.

Cost: SAR 5–10K + 20 hours. Reversible. Low-risk.
